## The idea (decided after research — do not re-litigate)
A **model-agnostic, post-hoc conformal wrapper** that turns any binary chest X-ray
abnormality classifier into a safe triage layer with a finite-sample guarantee:

- Auto-clear (call NORMAL, drop from worklist) a study iff score `s < t_low`.
- `t_low` calibrated so **missed-abnormal rate among cleared ≤ alpha** (radiologist
  picks alpha, e.g. 0.05). One-sided guarantee = patient-safety-critical side.
- Everything else stays on the worklist, ranked by score (highest suspicion first).

**Value proposition (MEASURED ON REAL DATA — supersedes the old simulated claim):**
at alpha=0.05, on 8,000 real NIH ChestX-ray14 studies (5,775 patients) scored by
torchxrayvision densenet121-res224-all (AUROC 0.746, prevalence 41.2%), under
patient-disjoint splits the layer cuts the worklist **15%** while its miss rate sits
on the bound (0.0497 vs alpha 0.05). Resampled to screening prevalence (5–10%
abnormal) that is **~19%**. NOTE: this is the *marginal* guarantee (~50% of
deployments land at/under alpha); calibrate_threshold_pac gives the
training-conditional version. See README.md for the full tables.

⚠️ The earlier "~63%" figure was a SIMULATION artifact — it came from a synthetic
classifier at AUROC 0.977. Do not quote it as a result. The honest framing is the
one that survives Q&A: *validity is model-agnostic and was confirmed on real data;
the payoff scales with base-model quality* (AUROC 0.90 → ~51% cut). Also note the
old wording conflated "63% of normal studies" with "63% of the worklist" — those
are different quantities. See README.md for the full tables.

## Why this idea (research summary)
- Radiologists don't need more *detection* AI; the real gaps are workflow,
  monitoring, and unreliable/miscalibrated confidence scores.
- Conformal prediction / selective triage is **published prior art** — cite it,
  don't claim it's novel. The contribution is a *packaged, runnable tool a
  non-ML radiologist can tune*, not new math.
- Prior work to cite:
  - "Conformal Triage for Medical Imaging AI Deployment", medRxiv 2024.
  - "Risk-Sensitive Conformal Prediction for Catheter Placement Detection in
    Chest X-rays", arXiv:2505.22496 (2025).
  - Vovk/Gammerman/Shafer, foundational conformal prediction.
- Honest framing for the writeup: "selective prediction exists in the literature;
  here is a practical, tunable triage layer that operationalizes it for real
  screening worklists."
